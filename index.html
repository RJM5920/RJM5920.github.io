个人简介 About Me

荣家明，现就读于中国科学院空天信息创新研究院，担任 IEEE GRSS UCAS 学生分会主席 ，导师为曾江源研究员。研究方向微波遥感定量反演，聚焦土壤水分（Soil Moisture, SM）与植被光学厚度（Vegetation Optical Depth, VOD），重点研究 L 波段被动微波 SM/VOD 反演算法，以及相关产品的时空填补及应用。

Jiaming Rong is currently studying at the Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Research Professor Jiangyuan Zeng. He serves as Chair of the IEEE GRSS UCAS Student Branch Chapter. His research focuses on quantitative microwave remote sensing, particularly soil moisture (SM) and vegetation optical depth (VOD). He primarily works on L-band passive microwave algorithms for SM/VOD retrieval, as well as spatiotemporal gap filling and applications of the resulting products.
